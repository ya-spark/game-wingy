# Privacy Policy for Wingy

_Last updated: 2026-10-28. Developer: Active Mind Craft. Contact: ggadmuser@gmail.com. Package: com.activemindcraft.wingy_

Wingy is a one-tap flyer game. It has no account system and no server of its own.

**What the app stores on your device:** your best score, coins, daily reward and streak progress, challenge and achievement progress, settings (sound, haptics, reduce motion, reminder) and purchase status (for example whether ads were removed). This data stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. It is not sent to us.

**Advertising:** Wingy shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): a banner on the home screen, interstitial ads after some game overs, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information and ad interaction data to serve, measure and protect against fraud in ads. The app declares the `AD_ID` permission for this. In the EEA, UK and other regions where required, we ask for your consent through Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested, and you can change your choice later in Settings under "Ad privacy options" when it is shown. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

**Purchases:** Purchases (remove ads, coin packs) are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive your payment details. The app checks your purchase status with Google Play to restore your purchases.

**Notifications:** If you turn on the daily reward reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No data is sent anywhere for this. You can turn it off in Settings or in Android notification settings.

**Google Play Games:** This version does not sign you in to Google Play Games and does not collect a player ID. If a later version adds Play Games sign-in (leaderboard, achievements), your player ID and scores will be handled by Google under Google's privacy policy, and this policy will be updated.

**Analytics and servers:** The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK and does not use Firebase. We do not operate a backend and do not collect, sell or share personal data ourselves.

**Children:** Wingy is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

**Your choices:** Reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Android settings; use "Ad privacy options" in Settings where shown; turn off reminders; uninstall the app to remove all local data.

**Changes:** We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
