**Title (<=30):** Wingy: One-Tap Moth Flyer

**Short description (<=80):** Tap to flap through crystal gaps. Chain perfect passes and blaze with Glow Dash.

**Full description (<=4000):**

Wingy is a one-tap flyer through a twilight canyon. Tap to flap your glowing moth through gaps
in crystal spires. Gaps change size and height, so no two runs feel alike.

- One-tap controls, quick restarts
- Perfect passes build streaks and a Bloom bonus
- Glow Dash: collect glow orbs, then blaze through the next spires and shatter them
- Daily rewards, streaks, three fresh daily challenges and achievements
- Plays offline, no account needed

Contains ads. You can remove them with a one-time purchase. Optional rewarded ads let you
continue once after a crash or refill Glow Dash.
