**Full description (<=4000):**

Wingy is a one-tap flyer through a twilight canyon. You are a small glowing moth crossing a canyon of crystal spires. Tap to flap, slip through the gaps and keep your balance between gravity and the next spire. Gaps change size and height, so no two runs feel alike.

HOW IT PLAYS
- One-tap controls and instant restarts: perfect for a quick 1 to 5 minute break
- Aim for the middle of each gap: Perfect passes build a streak and a Bloom bonus
- Glow Dash: collect glow orbs to fill the meter, then blaze through the next two spires and shatter them for bonus points
- Gaps that shrink, drift and breathe, with a wide breather every so often
- A twilight world that slowly shifts from dusk to aurora to midnight as your score grows
- Bronze, Silver, Gold and Prism medals for your best runs

COME BACK EVERY DAY
- Daily rewards on a 7-day cycle
- Play streaks, with streak freezes that cover a missed day
- Three fresh daily challenges
- Ten achievements to unlock
- Spend coins on Dash refills and streak freezes

FAIR AND SIMPLE
- Plays fully offline, no account needed
- No sign-up, no waiting, no energy timers
- Optional reminder for your daily reward (you choose in Settings)

Wingy contains ads. Remove banner and interstitial ads with a one-time purchase. Optional rewarded ads let you continue once after a crash or refill Glow Dash. You can also buy a coin pack, which is entirely optional.

Tap, flap, glow.
